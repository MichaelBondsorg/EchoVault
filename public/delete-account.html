Delete Your Engram Account

You can permanently delete your Engram account and all of the data associated with it at any time. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Delete from within the app (fastest)

  1. Open Engram and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Under Safety & Privacy, tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete everything.

Your account and data are erased right away and you are signed out.

Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email support@theengram.app from the address associated with your account and ask us to delete it. We will verify your identity and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes all of your data, including: your journal entries and voice transcripts, AI insights and detected patterns, goals and signals, safety plan, connected health/biometric data (e.g. Whoop), notification tokens, and your sign-in identity. None of it is retained after deletion.

Engram is a general-wellness self-reflection tool. It is not therapy, not a medical device, and not a crisis service. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (US Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).
