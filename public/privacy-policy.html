Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 6, 2026

Introduction

Engram ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

Engram is a general-wellness journaling tool for self-reflection. It is not therapy, not a medical device, and not a crisis service, and it does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. We take the privacy of your journaling and health data extremely seriously and never sell it or use it for advertising.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Journal Entries: Text entries, voice recordings, and transcriptions you create
  • Account Information: Email address and authentication data when you sign in with Google, Apple, or email & password (including optional two-factor authentication)
  • Mood Data: Mood ratings and emotional context you provide with entries
  • Safety Plans: Personal safety information, coping strategies, and emergency contacts you choose to store

Health, Biometric & Environmental Data (Optional)

If you choose to connect these sources, we collect and use them only to add context to your entries and correlations. This data is never used for advertising or shared with data brokers.

  • Wearable biometrics (WHOOP): recovery, strain, sleep, and heart-rate metrics, retrieved via WHOOP's API with your authorization
  • Apple Health / Google Fit: sleep, heart rate, steps, and related metrics you permit the app to read
  • Location: your approximate location (when granted) to attach weather/environmental context to an entry
  • Weather: local weather and daylight data retrieved from the Open-Meteo service using your location

Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: Features used, interaction patterns (stored locally and in your private Firebase account)
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version (for compatibility purposes only)
  • Crash & Diagnostics: crash reports and error diagnostics via Firebase Crashlytics to keep the app stable (we do not include your journal content in crash reports)

Information Processed by Third-Party AI Services

To provide AI-powered features, certain data is processed by third-party AI providers acting as our service providers. We ask for your permission before sending journal or health data to these services.

  • Voice Transcription: Audio recordings are sent to OpenAI (Whisper) to convert speech to text
  • Voice Conversations: When you use the AI companion by voice, audio and transcripts are streamed to OpenAI's real-time GPT-4o service to power the conversation
  • Entry Analysis & Insights: Journal entry text is sent to Google Gemini for mood analysis, pattern detection, and insight generation
  • AI Chat: Questions and relevant entry context you send to the AI assistant are processed by OpenAI and/or Google Gemini
  • Smart Search: Entry embeddings are generated using AI services for semantic search
Important: These AI providers process your data only to return a result to you. Under their API terms they do not use your content to train their models, and retention is limited to short abuse-monitoring windows (or none, where zero-retention processing is available). We are not a HIPAA-covered entity and do not describe ourselves as "HIPAA-compliant".

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Engram service
  • Transcribe your voice recordings into text
  • Generate AI-powered insights and mood analysis
  • Enable smart search across your journal entries
  • Detect potential crisis situations and provide safety resources
  • Improve and optimize the application

Data Storage and Security

Where Your Data Is Stored

  • Firebase Cloud Firestore: Your journal entries, mood data, and account preferences are stored in Google Firebase, isolated to your user account
  • Local Device: Some data may be cached locally for offline access

Security Measures

  • All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Firebase provides encryption at rest
  • Authentication is handled securely through Google Sign-In
  • Your data is isolated by your unique user ID

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information.

We never use your health, biometric, or journal data for advertising, and we do not share it with advertising networks or data brokers.

We may share your information only in these circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With infrastructure and AI service providers (Google Firebase, OpenAI, Google Gemini, WHOOP, Open-Meteo) solely to operate the features you use
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights
  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly authorize sharing

Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all your stored journal entries and data within the app
  • Delete: Delete individual entries, or permanently delete your entire account and all associated data from within the app (Settings → Safety & Privacy → Delete Account) or via our account deletion page
  • Export: Export your journal entries (PDF export feature)
  • Opt-Out: Stop using voice recording, health integrations, or AI features at any time

Data Retention

  • Your data is retained as long as you maintain an active account
  • You can delete your data at any time through the app
  • Upon account deletion, your data is permanently removed from our systems

Children's Privacy

Engram is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Mental Health Disclaimer

Engram is a journaling tool and is not a substitute for professional mental health care. If you are experiencing a mental health crisis:

  • United States: Call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline)
  • Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741
  • Emergency: Call 911 or your local emergency number
